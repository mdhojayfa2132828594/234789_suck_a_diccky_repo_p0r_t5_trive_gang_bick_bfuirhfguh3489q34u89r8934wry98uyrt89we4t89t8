Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. Overview

This application ("Nix Trive Channel Operations") is private automation tooling used by a single operator to manage YouTube channels that the operator owns. It is not offered to the public, has no customers, and does not collect data from visitors to this site.

2. Data accessed via the Google API

With the operator's explicit authorisation, the application requests the following YouTube Data API scopes:

  • youtube.upload — to publish videos to the operator's own channels
  • youtube.force-ssl — to read and post comments on the operator's own channels

These scopes are exercised only against channels owned by the operator. The application does not access, read or modify data belonging to any other channel or user.

3. Data processed

The application processes: video files, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, and comment text associated with the operator's own channels. Comment text written by viewers is read in order to generate responses and to identify topics for future videos.

4. Data storage

Operational data is stored in the operator's private source repository and associated encrypted secrets store. No data is transmitted to advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics providers.

5. Third-party processing

Comment text may be sent to third-party large language model APIs solely to generate a reply. These providers are contractually prohibited from training on submitted data where such an option is offered. No personally identifying information is deliberately included in such requests.

6. Data sharing and sale

No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

7. Data retention

Operational records are retained only as long as needed to operate the channels and may be deleted at any time by the operator.

8. Children's data

This application is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

Questions may be raised as an issue on the associated public source repository.
